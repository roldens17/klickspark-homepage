Questions

How much does U-Street cost?

$59, one-time. No subscription. The download is instant after purchase.

What do I get?

The theme .zip file plus an install guide.

How do I install it?

Upload the .zip to Shopify as-is. Do not unzip it first. The included install guide walks you through each step.

Is it an Online Store 2.0 theme?

Yes. U-Street is built for Online Store 2.0 and comes with 20 sections.

Can I try it before buying?

When a shopper adds to bag, a small popup cart confirms the item. Product cards also have quick view.

Three: Street Black, Concrete and Varsity.
