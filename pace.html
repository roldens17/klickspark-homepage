02 — Features

What it does

  • Spec sheet

    Product tags become the spec sheet. spec:Cushion:4 draws a rating bar. stat:Weight:212 g draws a stat box. The same tags show on the product page and the homepage.

  • Mega menu

    A desktop mega menu with columns from your navigation, plus up to 2 promo tiles.

  • 3 presets

    Volt, Ember and Glacier. Volt is the default: true black with lime buttons.

  • Shop by Activity

    Homepage tiles that open a collection for each activity, such as run or train.

  • Drop countdown

    A countdown section for the next launch, with an email signup.

  • Size guide

    A product-specific size guide. Footwear can use its own guide, separate from apparel.

  • Quick View

    Open a product from its card, pick a variant, and add it without leaving the grid.

  • Popup cart

    Adding to bag opens a popup cart. Shoppers can change quantity, remove a line, and check out from there.

  • Sticky add to cart

    On phones, a sticky add-to-cart bar stays on screen while the product page scrolls.

  • Filters and sort

    Collection and search pages can show storefront filters and sort.

  • Online Store 2.0

    Sections in the theme editor, including app blocks on the product section.

  • Lighthouse

    On the demo, Lighthouse averages about 98 for performance and about 99 for accessibility.

What do I get?

How do the spec tags work?

Add tags on the product. spec:Cushion:4 becomes a rating bar scored 4 out of 5. stat:Weight:212 g becomes a stat box. PACE draws them on the product page and on the homepage spec sheet. No app and no code. The buyer guide has the full rules.

Which preset should I start with?

Volt is the default: true black with lime buttons. Ember is warm bone and orange. Glacier is icy blue. Upload the zip named for the preset you want.

Is it an Online Store 2.0 theme?

Yes. You edit it with sections in the theme editor, and the product section accepts app blocks.

How does the cart work?

When a shopper adds to bag, a popup cart confirms the item. They can change quantity and check out from the popup cart. Product cards also have Quick View.

What about phones?

The product page has a sticky add-to-cart bar on small screens. Collection pages support filters and sort.
